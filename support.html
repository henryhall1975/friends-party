Preguntas frecuentes

  • ¿Cómo borro mi cuenta? Dentro de la app, ve a tu perfil y toca "Borrar mi cuenta y mis datos".
  • ¿Cómo reporto o bloqueo a alguien? Entra a su perfil y toca "Reportar" o "Bloquear".
  • ¿Cómo restauro una compra? En la Tienda, toca "Restaurar compras".
  • ¿Dónde veo qué datos guarda la app? En nuestra Política de privacidad.

Te respondemos lo antes posible al correo de arriba.

Support

Friends Party

Have a question, a problem, or want to report something about the app? Write to us directly:

friendspartysupport@gmail.com

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to your profile and tap "Delete my account and my data".
  • How do I report or block someone? Go to their profile and tap "Report" or "Block".
  • How do I restore a purchase? In the Shop, tap "Restore purchases".
  • Where can I see what data the app stores? In our Privacy Policy.

We'll reply as soon as possible to the email above.

  • 앱이 어떤 정보를 저장하는지 어디서 볼 수 있나요? 개인정보 처리방침에서 확인할 수 있습니다.
